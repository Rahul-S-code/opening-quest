About Opening Quest

Chess openings with Grandpa Rook

Opening Quest is a free chess app that teaches children the openings. It was started by Rahul, a parent who wanted their own child to understand why each opening move matters, not just memorise it.

How it works

  • Lessons. 50 openings, 25 for White and 25 for Black. Grandpa Rook explains every move out loud. If your child tries something else, he says what they would be missing.
  • Puzzles. Positions from each opening and famous opening traps, with a daily review that brings back the tricky ones.
  • Play a game. After a lesson, your child can keep playing the same game against the computer, at Novice, Improver or Good player level.
  • Progress. Stars, think-ahead stickers and practice levels from New to Master.

Grandpa Rook's voice

Grandpa Rook's lines are recorded with an AI voice from ElevenLabs. Lessons that aren't recorded yet use the voice built into your device.

Free, and no ads

There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Logging in is optional, and it only saves your child's progress.
