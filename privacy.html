Privacy notice

Last updated 2 November 2026

Opening Quest is made for children, so we collect as little as possible. This notice explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written for parents and guardians.

Who we are

Opening Quest is run by Rahul, an individual, as a free, non-commercial project. Rahul is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach Rahul through the contact details at the end of this notice.

Playing as a guest

You can use every lesson, puzzle and game without an account. Guest progress and settings (such as voice on or off) are saved only in your browser's local storage on that device. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

If a parent logs in

A parent or guardian can create an account with an email address and password, so progress is saved across devices. We store:

  • The parent's email address and a securely hashed password. These are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see the password.
  • The child's learning progress. This covers lessons and puzzles completed, stars, stickers, practice counts, game results and the date of the last change. It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, and only the signed-in account can read it.

We do not ask for a child's name, age, photo, location or contact details, and there is nowhere in the app to enter them. Accounts are meant to be created by a parent or guardian, not by a child.

We use this information only to save and restore progress, and to send a password-reset email when you ask for one. Our lawful basis is providing the service you asked for (contract) and, for the child's progress, the parent's consent when creating the account.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No chat, messaging or contact between users.
  • No profiling or automated decisions about your child.

Services that see technical data

Like any website, a few services receive your device's IP address and basic browser information when the page loads:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts.
  • Google Firebase handles accounts and saved progress, and only if you log in.

Grandpa Rook's voice clips are pre-recorded files hosted with the website. Nothing your child does is sent to the voice provider. These services may process data outside the UK and EEA under their own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. If an account isn't used for 2 years, we may delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the data we hold for your account, and you can withdraw consent at any time. To do this, contact us using the details below, from the email address on the account. We will reply within one month. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects saved accounts in a meaningful way, we will say so in the app.

Contact

Please open an issue on GitHub. Don't include any personal details in a public issue. Just ask for a private reply, and we'll arrange one.
